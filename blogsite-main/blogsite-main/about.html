Why this blog exists

This site started as a class project — a shared blog we were asked to design, then refactor with a real component framework once the wireframe was locked. Rebuilding it in Bootstrap forced us to think about the layout as a system: one navbar, one grid, one set of colours, reused everywhere instead of styled by hand on every page.

What began as an assignment became something we kept adding to, because the constraint was useful: fewer decisions per page meant more attention left over for the writing itself.

What we care about

We'd rather publish four solid essays a month than twelve thin ones. Every post here goes through at least one full rewrite, and we're comfortable sitting on an idea for a week if it isn't ready.

How we work

Three of us split writing, editing, and the site itself. We meet briefly on Mondays to plan the fortnight, and the rest of the schedule is quiet on purpose — no dashboards, no daily standup, just a shared doc and a deadline.
